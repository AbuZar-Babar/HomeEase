{\centering\bfseries\underline{EXECUTIVE SUMMARY}\par}
\vspace{8pt}

TrekPal is a multi-platform travel management and marketplace system developed to address the inefficiencies of fragmented trip planning. In traditional travel workflows, users often rely on separate services for accommodation, transportation, and itinerary coordination, resulting in duplicated effort, limited pricing transparency, and weak trust in service providers.\par


TrekPal solves this by unifying travelers, travel agencies, hotels, and platform administrators in a single digital ecosystem.\par


The system introduces a bid-based trip planning model. Instead of only selecting fixed listings, travelers create structured trip requests with destination, dates, budget, and group size. Travel agencies respond with competitive bids that may include hotel, transport, and package components. This approach increases price competitiveness, supports customization, and improves decision quality for users. The platform also supports direct inventory browsing, booking management, and role-based interactions for operational control.\par


TrekPal consists of four integrated components: (1) a Flutter traveler application, (2) a React-based agency portal, (3) a React-based hotel portal, and (4) a React-based admin portal, all connected to a centralized backend built with Node.js, Express, and TypeScript. The backend uses PostgreSQL (via Prisma) for relational data management, JWT/Supabase-based authentication, and Socket.IO for real-time features such as chat and live booking/bid updates.\par


The project follows an Object-Oriented, modular architecture with iterative Agile development. Core modules include authentication and verification, trip request lifecycle, bidding, booking, inventory (hotels/rooms/vehicles), package offers, and administrative approvals. This modular design supports maintainability and future feature expansion.\par


The outcome is a working end-to-end travel platform that improves transparency, coordination, and service quality in trip planning. TrekPal demonstrates how a unified, role-driven marketplace can modernize travel operations while providing a scalable foundation for future enhancements such as intelligent recommendations, automated offer ranking, stronger analytics, and broader third-party integrations.\par

\section{Introduction}

\ThesisParagraph{Travel planning in many markets remains fragmented across disconnected service providers, platforms, and communication channels. Travelers often need to separately search for hotels, transportation, and package options, then manually compare prices and coordinate with agencies or vendors. This process is time-consuming, lacks transparency, and frequently leads to inconsistent user experiences. Existing direct-booking platforms improve accessibility, but they generally follow static listing models that provide limited support for personalized negotiation and integrated multi-service trip planning.}


\ThesisParagraph{TrekPal is developed to address these limitations through a unified, multi-role travel marketplace. The platform connects travelers, travel agencies, hotels, and administrators in a single digital system where trip planning, bidding, booking, and coordination can be managed end-to-end. Its core differentiator is a structured trip-request and bid workflow: travelers define trip requirements (destination, dates, budget, and group size), and agencies respond with competitive offers that can combine accommodation, transport, and related services.}


\ThesisParagraph{The system is implemented as a multi-platform architecture consisting of:}

\subsection{Literature Review}

\ThesisParagraph{In Pakistan, digital travel adoption has increased through social media, messaging apps, and online listing platforms, but end-to-end trip planning is still largely fragmented. Travelers commonly use different channels for hotels, transport, and package coordination, often relying on WhatsApp, Facebook pages, informal agents, and separate booking websites. This creates delays, inconsistent pricing, and limited service transparency, especially for domestic tourism routes such as Northern Areas, Murree/Galiyat, Swat, and coastal destinations.}


\ThesisParagraph{Most currently used solutions in the local market follow a listing and inquiry model rather than a fully integrated transactional workflow. Users browse options, contact providers manually, negotiate separately, and finalize arrangements through calls/messages. Research and market observations in similar developing-platform contexts show that such workflows increase operational friction and reduce comparability between competing offers, particularly when users need bundled services (hotel + transport + itinerary support).}


\ThesisParagraph{A key issue highlighted in the Pakistan context is trust and verification. Travelers often face uncertainty about provider legitimacy, service quality, and booking reliability. In informal or semi-digital arrangements, listing authenticity, cancellation handling, and post-booking accountability are not always clear. This aligns with broader literature on emerging digital marketplaces, where identity verification, moderation, and provider approval mechanisms significantly improve platform credibility and user confidence.}


\ThesisParagraph{Another relevant local trend is the growth of mobile-first usage. A large segment of Pakistani users accesses services primarily through smartphones, making app usability, low-friction workflows, and real-time communication essential. Systems that support instant updates, chat-based coordination, and transparent status tracking are better aligned with local user behavior than static, desktop-oriented booking flows.}


\subsection{Analysis from Literature Review}

\ThesisParagraph{The literature and Pakistan-focused market context indicate that existing travel solutions are largely effective for listing visibility but weak in integrated, end-to-end trip coordination. Most workflows remain fragmented across separate providers and communication channels, with travelers manually handling comparison, negotiation, and confirmation. This directly validates TrekPal's problem framing and design direction.}


\ThesisParagraph{In relation to the reviewed systems, TrekPal differs in the following important ways:}


\ThesisParagraph{\textbf{From static listing to demand-driven planning}}


\ThesisParagraph{Existing platforms are mostly supply-led (users browse what is listed). TrekPal uses a demand-led flow where travelers submit structured trip requests and agencies respond with targeted bids.}


\ThesisParagraph{\textbf{From isolated services to bundled offers}}


\ThesisParagraph{Conventional workflows often separate accommodation and transport decisions. TrekPal enables agencies to propose combined offers (hotel + transport + related services), reducing coordination burden for travelers.}


\ThesisParagraph{\textbf{From informal negotiation to structured competition}}


\ThesisParagraph{In local practice, price negotiation often occurs privately via calls/messages, making comparison difficult. TrekPal formalizes this into comparable bid submissions, improving transparency and decision quality.}


\ThesisParagraph{\textbf{From weak trust signals to controlled verification}}


\ThesisParagraph{Literature emphasizes trust as a major constraint in digital travel marketplaces. TrekPal incorporates role-based verification and admin approval workflows for agencies/hotels/vehicles, improving reliability and platform governance.}


\ThesisParagraph{\textbf{From delayed communication to real-time interaction}}


\ThesisParagraph{Local travel coordination relies heavily on messaging, but without structured system state. TrekPal integrates real-time chat and event updates (e.g., bid/booking state changes), aligning with mobile-first usage behavior.}


\ThesisParagraph{\textbf{From disconnected operations to platform-level lifecycle management}}


\ThesisParagraph{Existing user journeys often end at booking. TrekPal supports a broader lifecycle: request creation, bidding, offer acceptance, booking tracking, and post-booking interaction..}


\subsection{Methodology and Software Lifecycle for This Project}

\ThesisParagraph{The development of TrekPal follows an~\textbf{Agile Scrum-based software lifecycle}~combined with a~\textbf{modular, layered, and object-oriented system design}. This approach was selected because the project includes multiple independent yet connected products (traveler app, agency portal, hotel portal, admin portal, and backend services), each with evolving requirements and frequent cross-module integration.}


\ThesisParagraph{The software lifecycle was executed in iterative phases rather than a strict linear model:}


\begin{list}{}{\setlength{\leftmargin}{\dimexpr\ThesisParagraphIndent+1.5em\relax}\setlength{\labelsep}{0.5em}\setlength{\topsep}{2pt}\setlength{\parsep}{0pt}}
\item[1.] \textbf{Requirement Identification and Scope Definition}\newline{} Core actors (Traveler, Agency, Hotel, Admin) and major workflows (trip requests, bids, bookings, inventory management, approvals, and communication) were finalized from domain analysis.
\end{list}


\begin{list}{}{\setlength{\leftmargin}{\dimexpr\ThesisParagraphIndent+1.5em\relax}\setlength{\labelsep}{0.5em}\setlength{\topsep}{2pt}\setlength{\parsep}{0pt}}
\item[2.] \textbf{System Design and Architecture Planning}\newline{} A role-based architecture was designed with a centralized backend API and separate frontend clients. Domain entities (User, Agency, Hotel, Room, Vehicle, TripRequest, Bid, Package, Booking, Review, Chat/Group) were modeled and relational dependencies were defined in the database schema.
\end{list}


\begin{list}{}{\setlength{\leftmargin}{\dimexpr\ThesisParagraphIndent+1.5em\relax}\setlength{\labelsep}{0.5em}\setlength{\topsep}{2pt}\setlength{\parsep}{0pt}}
\item[3.] \textbf{Incremental Implementation (Sprint-Based)}\newline{} Development proceeded feature-by-feature in short iterations. Each sprint targeted a specific vertical slice (e.g., auth, trip requests, bidding, booking flow, admin approvals, room management), enabling early validation and controlled integration.
\end{list}


\begin{list}{}{\setlength{\leftmargin}{\dimexpr\ThesisParagraphIndent+1.5em\relax}\setlength{\labelsep}{0.5em}\setlength{\topsep}{2pt}\setlength{\parsep}{0pt}}
\item[4.] \textbf{Continuous Integration and Refactoring}\newline{} As modules matured, API contracts, validation rules, and role-guard logic were refined. Cross-portal consistency issues were resolved through iterative fixes and backend-centralized enforcement.
\end{list}


\begin{list}{}{\setlength{\leftmargin}{\dimexpr\ThesisParagraphIndent+1.5em\relax}\setlength{\labelsep}{0.5em}\setlength{\topsep}{2pt}\setlength{\parsep}{0pt}}
\item[5.] \textbf{Testing and Stabilization}\newline{} Manual and code-level verification was used throughout development to validate role-specific workflows, state transitions, and failure handling (e.g., unauthorized actions, booking constraints, missing profiles, and edge-case payloads).
\end{list}

\section{Problem Definition}

\ThesisParagraph{This chapter defines the primary challenges addressed by the proposed travel management system.}


\subsection{Problem Statement}

\ThesisParagraph{In Pakistan's current travel ecosystem, planning and managing a trip is still fragmented across multiple disconnected channels. Travelers often use separate sources for hotels, transport, and agency coordination, including social media pages, messaging apps, informal agents, and listing websites. This creates inefficiency, inconsistent service quality, weak price comparability, and high coordination effort for end users.}


\ThesisParagraph{Most commonly used digital travel options in this context are listing-driven. Users browse predefined options and then negotiate or confirm details outside the platform. Such systems do not provide a structured mechanism for travelers to express complete trip needs and receive comparable, competitive offers from multiple service providers. As a result, pricing remains static or opaque, customization is limited, and users have reduced control over end-to-end trip decisions.}


\ThesisParagraph{A second critical issue is trust. In semi-digital and informal arrangements, verification of agencies, hotels, and transport providers is often weak. Travelers may face uncertainty regarding provider authenticity, booking reliability, cancellation handling, and service accountability. At the same time, service providers lack a standardized digital workflow to respond to demand, manage inventory, and coordinate bookings efficiently.}


\ThesisParagraph{These gaps highlight the need for a centralized, role-driven travel management platform that supports integrated planning, structured bidding, secure booking workflows, and verification controls. The system must:}


\begin{list}{}{\setlength{\leftmargin}{\dimexpr\ThesisParagraphIndent+1.5em\relax}\setlength{\labelsep}{0.5em}\setlength{\topsep}{2pt}\setlength{\parsep}{0pt}}
\item[\textbullet] Unify key travel services (trip requests, offers, hotels, transport, bookings, and communication).
\end{list}


\begin{list}{}{\setlength{\leftmargin}{\dimexpr\ThesisParagraphIndent+1.5em\relax}\setlength{\labelsep}{0.5em}\setlength{\topsep}{2pt}\setlength{\parsep}{0pt}}
\item[\textbullet] Enable dynamic interaction through a bid-based marketplace instead of only static listings.
\end{list}


\begin{list}{}{\setlength{\leftmargin}{\dimexpr\ThesisParagraphIndent+1.5em\relax}\setlength{\labelsep}{0.5em}\setlength{\topsep}{2pt}\setlength{\parsep}{0pt}}
\item[\textbullet] Provide transparent, comparable offers aligned with traveler constraints.
\end{list}


\begin{list}{}{\setlength{\leftmargin}{\dimexpr\ThesisParagraphIndent+1.5em\relax}\setlength{\labelsep}{0.5em}\setlength{\topsep}{2pt}\setlength{\parsep}{0pt}}
\item[\textbullet] Improve trust through approval and moderation workflows.
\end{list}


\begin{list}{}{\setlength{\leftmargin}{\dimexpr\ThesisParagraphIndent+1.5em\relax}\setlength{\labelsep}{0.5em}\setlength{\topsep}{2pt}\setlength{\parsep}{0pt}}
\item[\textbullet] Support real-time coordination among travelers and service providers.
\end{list}

\subsection{Algorithm}

\subsubsection{Room Availability Resolver}

\ThesisParagraph{This algorithm verifies if a hotel room has free capacity for all nights in a selected trip date range. It checks individual date availability slots and returns false if any date is fully booked.}


{\setstretch{1}\fontsize{11}{13}\selectfont\raggedright\setlength{\parskip}{1pt}
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Input: roomId, startDate, endDate\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Output: available (Boolean)\par
\smallskip
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Set start = StartOfDay(startDate), end = StartOfDay(endDate)\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Compute nights = Ceiling((end - start) / 1 Day)\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If nights <= 0: Return False\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Query RoomAvailability database records where roomId == roomId and date is in [start, end-1]\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If length(records) < nights: Return False (availability slots not generated)\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}For each record in records:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If record.available < 1:\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}Return False (room is fully occupied on at least one night)\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Return True\par
}


\subsubsection{Bidding Negotiation State Machine}

\ThesisParagraph{TrekPal implements a structured negotiation state machine between travelers and travel agencies. Whenever a traveler or agency makes a counteroffer, the algorithm checks the current turn, inserts a new revision, and toggles the awaitingActionBy flag.}


{\setstretch{1}\fontsize{11}{13}\selectfont\raggedright\setlength{\parskip}{1pt}
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Input: bidId, actorRole (TRAVELER or AGENCY), counterPrice, counterDescription, counterOfferDetails\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Output: updatedBid (Object)\par
\smallskip
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Query Bid record by bidId from database\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If bid.status != 'PENDING': Throw Error ('Only pending bids can be revised')\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If actorRole == TRAVELER:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Verify bid.tripRequest.userId == actorId\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If bid.awaitingActionBy != 'TRAVELER': Throw Error ('Not your turn to counteroffer')\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Set nextAwaitingAction = 'AGENCY'\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Else if actorRole == AGENCY:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Verify bid.agencyId == actorId\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If bid.awaitingActionBy != 'AGENCY': Throw Error ('Not your turn to counteroffer')\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Set nextAwaitingAction = 'TRAVELER'\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Run database Transaction:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Create BidRevision record with counterPrice, counterDescription, and counterOfferDetails\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Update Bid record: set price = counterPrice, description = counterDescription, awaitingActionBy = nextAwaitingAction\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Emit real-time notification to the counter-party via Socket.IO\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Return updated Bid object\par
}


\subsubsection{Package Offer Seat Allocation \& Transactional Booking Lock}

\ThesisParagraph{To prevent race conditions during concurrent bookings of limited-seat tour packages, TrekPal executes a SQL row-level lock (FOR UPDATE) on the package table, counts confirmed seats, validates limits, and atomically creates a confirmed booking inside a single database transaction.}


{\setstretch{1}\fontsize{11}{13}\selectfont\raggedright\setlength{\parskip}{1pt}
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Input: bookingId, packageId\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Output: confirmedBooking (Object)\par
\smallskip
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Start Database Transaction:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Query Package record by packageId\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Execute SQL raw lock: 'SELECT id FROM packages WHERE id = packageId FOR UPDATE'\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Count bookings where packageId == packageId and status == 'CONFIRMED'\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Retrieve package room allocations and compute packageCapacity (minimum of maxSeats and reservedRooms)\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If confirmedBookingsCount >= packageCapacity:\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}Throw Error ('This trip offer is sold out or has no reserved rooms left')\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Query availability for package vehicleId and driverId in [package.startDate, package.endDate]\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If vehicle/driver conflicts exist:\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}Throw Error ('Transport resources are unavailable')\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Update Booking record for bookingId: set status = 'CONFIRMED'\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Commit Transaction\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Emit real-time booking update via Socket.IO\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Return confirmed Booking object\par
}


\subsubsection{Dedicated vs. Transfer-Based Vehicle Allocation}

\ThesisParagraph{This algorithm determines vehicle availability based on occupancy modes. A dedicated vehicle is occupied for the full trip duration, while a transfer vehicle is only occupied on pick-up and drop-off days, resolving scheduling conflicts accordingly.}


{\setstretch{1}\fontsize{11}{13}\selectfont\raggedright\setlength{\parskip}{1pt}
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Input: vehicleId, driverId, startDate, endDate, dedicatedVehicle (Boolean)\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Output: occupiedDateRanges (Array of Date Ranges)\par
\smallskip
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If dedicatedVehicle == True:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}occupiedDateRanges = [ [startDate, endDate] ] (occupies the full range)\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Else (dedicatedVehicle == False - Transfer Mode):\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Set departureEnd = startDate + 1 Day\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Set returnStart = endDate - 1 Day\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}occupiedDateRanges = [ [startDate, departureEnd] ] (occupies departure day)\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If returnStart > startDate:\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}Append [returnStart, endDate] to occupiedDateRanges (occupies return day)\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}For each range in occupiedDateRanges:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Query Booking table where status == 'CONFIRMED' and vehicleId == vehicleId\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If any confirmed booking overlaps range:\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}Throw Conflict Error ('Selected vehicle is already booked for these dates')\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Query Booking table where status == 'CONFIRMED' and driverId == driverId\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If any confirmed booking overlaps range:\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}Throw Conflict Error ('Assigned driver is already booked for these dates')\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Return occupiedDateRanges\par
}


\subsubsection{Transactional Bid Acceptance \& Multi-Bid Auto-Resolution}

\ThesisParagraph{This algorithm atomicizes the process of a traveler accepting a custom bid. It locks hotel room availability, transitions the accepted bid to 'ACCEPTED', automatically rejects all other competing bids for the same trip, updates the trip request status, snapshot-allocates transportation assets, and creates a confirmed booking record all in a single database transaction.}


{\setstretch{1}\fontsize{11}{13}\selectfont\raggedright\setlength{\parskip}{1pt}
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Input: bidId, travelerId\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Output: resolvedBooking (Object)\par
\smallskip
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Query Bid record by bidId from database\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If bid.status != 'PENDING': Throw Error ('Bid is no longer pending')\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If bid.tripRequest.userId != travelerId: Throw Error ('Unauthorized')\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If bid.tripRequest.status != 'PENDING': Throw Error ('Trip request is no longer accepting bids')\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If bid.awaitingActionBy == 'AGENCY': Throw Error ('Cannot accept bid under agency review')\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Start Database Transaction:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If tripRequest contains roomId, startDate, and endDate:\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}Verify and decrement availability for roomId in [startDate, endDate] by 1\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Set accepted bid.status = 'ACCEPTED' and awaitingActionBy = 'NONE'\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}For each competing bid where tripRequestId == bid.tripRequestId and bidId != acceptedBidId:\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}Set status = 'REJECTED' and awaitingActionBy = 'NONE'\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Update TripRequest status to 'ACCEPTED'\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Resolve transport asset prioritization: set resolvedVehicleId = bid.vehicleId or tripRequest.vehicleId\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Query driverId from vehicle table and assert vehicle/driver availability for resolvedVehicleId\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Create Booking record with status = 'CONFIRMED', totalAmount = bid.price, and vehicle/driver details snapshot\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Commit Transaction\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Emit Socket.IO update notifications to traveler and winning agency\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Return created Booking object\par
}


\subsubsection{Unified Hotel Occupancy \& Reservation Aggregator}

\ThesisParagraph{This algorithm aggregates and formats booking data from two different tables (Direct Traveler Bookings and bulk Agency Package Allocations) into a unified chronological feed. This allows hotel operators to view their overall real-time room occupancy across both individual trips and tour packages.}


{\setstretch{1}\fontsize{11}{13}\selectfont\raggedright\setlength{\parskip}{1pt}
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Input: hotelId, filters (status, page, limit)\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Output: paginatedMergedOccupancyList (Array of Objects)\par
\smallskip
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Query database for Direct Bookings where (hotelId == hotelId or room.hotelId == hotelId) and matches status filter\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Query database for Package Room Allocations (packageRoomAllocation) where room.hotelId == hotelId\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Set directItems = Array of Direct Bookings mapped to a common format (source = 'DIRECT\_BOOKING', status = booking.status)\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Set packageItems = Array of Package Room Allocations mapped to a common format:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Set startDate = package.startDate, endDate = package.startDate + package.duration Days\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Format: source = 'PACKAGE\_RESERVATION', status = (package.isActive ? 'RESERVED' : 'HELD'), reservedRooms = allocation.reservedRooms\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Merge directItems and packageItems into a single list: mergedItems\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Sort mergedItems in descending order by createdAt timestamp\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If status filter is active, filter mergedItems by matching status value\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Paginate: slice mergedItems based on page and limit offsets\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Return sliced list and total count\par
}


\subsubsection{Safe Tour Offer Modification \& Room Inventory Hold Manager}

\ThesisParagraph{This algorithm secures updates to active packages. Before allowing changes to details such as room plans, dates, or status, it ensures that room allocations are not reduced below the count of already confirmed bookings. It then releases old room allocations and locks new allocations dynamically inside a transaction.}


{\setstretch{1}\fontsize{11}{13}\selectfont\raggedright\setlength{\parskip}{1pt}
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Input: packageId, agencyId, updatePayload (dates, duration, roomPlan, activeStatus)\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Output: updatedPackage (Object)\par
\smallskip
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Query existing Package record by packageId and agencyId\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If updatePayload specifies activeStatus == True:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Validate that hotel, startDate, and roomPlan are present\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Validate that hotels, rooms, and vehicles belong to approved marketplace\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Start Database Transaction:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Execute row lock: 'SELECT id FROM packages WHERE id = packageId FOR UPDATE'\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Count bookings for this package where status is in ['PENDING', 'CONFIRMED']\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If confirmed bookings exist:\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}If new roomPlan is empty or any room's count < confirmedCount:\par
\noindent\hangindent=4.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{3.00em}Throw Error ('Cannot reduce allocations below confirmed traveler count')\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Get current room allocations and release held rooms in old date window\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If package has active status and new roomPlan is specified:\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}Compute new reservation date window: [startDate, startDate + duration]\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}Assert and deduct availability for new roomPlan in new window\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If active bookings exist:\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}Update all associated pending/confirmed Booking records to match new dates/hotel/transport\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Replace room allocation records in packageRoomAllocation table\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Update Package record with new payload details\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Commit Transaction\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Emit package update notifications via Socket.IO\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Return updated Package object\par
}


\subsubsection{Automated Rolling 365-Day Room Inventory Grid Initialization}

\ThesisParagraph{Whenever a hotel room is created or its quantity changes, this algorithm automates the generation of a one-year rolling inventory grid in the database. It uses database upserts to preserve custom capacity overrides while initializing new days.}


{\setstretch{1}\fontsize{11}{13}\selectfont\raggedright\setlength{\parskip}{1pt}
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Input: roomId, totalQuantity\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Output: None\par
\smallskip
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Set today = StartOfDay(CurrentDate)\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Create empty list: availabilities\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}For i = 0 to 364:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Set date = today + i Days\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Append \{ roomId = roomId, date = date, available = totalQuantity \} to availabilities\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}For each slot in availabilities:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Execute Database Upsert:\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}Match by composite key: roomId and date\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}If exists: update capacity 'available' = totalQuantity\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}If not exists: create record with roomId, date, and totalQuantity\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Return Success\par
}


\subsubsection{Dynamic Age Derivation \& Secure Profile Attachment Resolver}

\ThesisParagraph{This algorithm processes user profile metadata for traveler group lists. It derives the traveler's exact age in years based on birth date boundaries and checks if profile avatars require secure signature parameters for storage access.}


{\setstretch{1}\fontsize{11}{13}\selectfont\raggedright\setlength{\parskip}{1pt}
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Input: userRecord (name, dateOfBirth, avatarPath)\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Output: participantProfile (Object)\par
\smallskip
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Set initials = buildInitials(userRecord.name)\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Set age = Null\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If userRecord.dateOfBirth is not Null:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Set now = CurrentDate()\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Set age = now.year - dateOfBirth.year\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Set birthdayPassed = (now.month > dateOfBirth.month) OR \par
\noindent\hangindent=7.25em\hangafter=1\hspace*{6.25em}(now.month == dateOfBirth.month AND now.day >= dateOfBirth.day)\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If birthdayPassed == False:\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}Set age = age - 1\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Set avatarUrl = Null\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If userRecord.avatarPath is not Null:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If IsHttpUrl(userRecord.avatarPath) == True:\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}avatarUrl = userRecord.avatarPath\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Else:\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}Try:\par
\noindent\hangindent=4.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{3.00em}avatarUrl = GenerateSignedTemporaryUrl(userRecord.avatarPath)\par
\noindent\hangindent=3.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{2.00em}Catch:\par
\noindent\hangindent=4.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{3.00em}avatarUrl = Null\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Return \{ initials = initials, age = age, avatar = avatarUrl \}\par
}


\subsubsection{Authorized Room-Joining \& Real-Time Sync Engine}

\ThesisParagraph{This algorithm establishes socket-level security for TrekPal's real-time messaging services. Before joining a client to a WebSocket room, it verifies their JWT signature and queries their booking or package status in the database to prevent unauthorized message intercepts.}


{\setstretch{1}\fontsize{11}{13}\selectfont\raggedright\setlength{\parskip}{1pt}
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Input: clientSocket, connectionInfo (userId, role, roomId)\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Output: connectionResult (Object)\par
\smallskip
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Verify JWT signature of traveler or agency auth token on socket connection\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If invalid: Terminate socket connection\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If role == TRAVELER:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Query Booking record where userId == userId, package.tripGroup.id == roomId, and status == 'CONFIRMED'\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If no booking exists: Throw Error ('Unauthorized to join chat room')\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}If role == AGENCY:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Query Package record where agency.authUid == socket.authUid and tripGroup.id == roomId\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}If no package exists: Throw Error ('Unauthorized to join chat room')\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Join clientSocket to Socket.IO room identifier: roomId\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Listen for 'SEND\_MESSAGE' event:\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Validate message content length\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Create ChatMessage record in database linked to tripGroup and sender\par
\noindent\hangindent=2.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{1.00em}Broadcast 'RECEIVE\_MESSAGE' payload to all participants in roomId\par
\noindent\hangindent=1.00em\hangafter=1\hspace*{0.00em}Return success\par
}
